WE ARE ABLE

Empowering people with disabilities through skills, opportunities and sustainable sources of income.

Our Mission

We believe that everyone has abilities, talents and something valuable to contribute. WE ARE ABLE aims to support people with disabilities by helping them learn useful skills, create products and build opportunities for a better and more independent future.

What We Do

Learn Skills

We provide opportunities to learn practical and useful skills.

Create Products

Participants can use their skills to create products and show their abilities.

Earn Income

We help create opportunities to sell products and generate a source of income.
