🌟 OUR MISSION

WE ARE ABLE is a social project that helps people with disabilities learn practical skills, express their creativity and create products that can provide income opportunities.

We believe everyone has talents and abilities that deserve to be recognized. ❤️

👔 CEO

HASAN AWWAD

Founder & CEO of WE ARE ABLE

❤️ OUR STORY

This project began with a simple wish: to help people with disabilities learn practical skills and have more opportunities to earn income.

I have seen how difficult it can sometimes be for people to feel accepted and recognized for their abilities.

One meaningful moment stayed with me: I met a person with a disability who thanked me deeply when I shared this idea. That moment strengthened my belief that opportunity, skills and inclusion can make a real difference.

That is how WE ARE ABLE was born. ❤️
